RAG systems empower LLMs with external knowledge by providing a vector database and retrieving information as context for question answering, followed by the model citing this evidence and verifying whether the citations support its conclusions.
A key challenge is evidence completeness rather than just correctness: for instance, in Q1, the retrieved rule itself was accurate but missed crucial details like opening hours. An interesting next step is to combine keyword and embedding retrieval.
